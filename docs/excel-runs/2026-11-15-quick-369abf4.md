## Sheet review

The review ran **Clear outputs**, checked and pictured every sheet, ran the
demonstration portfolio and did the same again. In both states every sheet
passed every check against
[`docs/workbook/sheet-style.json`](../workbook/sheet-style.json): the font
(Aptos) and vertical centering of every used cell, no cell showing `####`,
dates only as `yyyy-mm-dd`, gridlines off, and the tab color of the sheet's
role.

Each of the 38 pictures was also looked at against the style rules and the
findings of #708. They show:

- every table header bold white 11 pt on navy (`1F3864`), each header row tall
  enough for its wrapped text;
- Params with the day counts as integers and the IR bucket correlations as
  percentages; TradeCalc with the commodity option's lambda as a number;
- the counterparty descriptions of NettingSets and Results, the CSV field names
  of the staging sheets and the Checks note wrapped, with taller rows;
- the title bands of NettingSets and Results to the end of their table;
- the absolute tolerance of BankNettingSets and BankTrades centered;
- TestCatalogue reading NOT RUN, with no color, after Clear outputs, and 28 of
  28 PASS after the run;
- the README sheet titled README.

Two things are left as they are, by the owner's decision: the CSV field names,
which have no spaces, break inside the name; and the AutoFilter buttons of
Trades and Basel_vs_CRR cover the last letter of some headers, as before #708.
