# Workbook screenshots

`SACCR_NettingSets.png`, `SACCR_Trades.png`, `SACCR_Params.png`,
`SACCR_Params_Supervisory.png`, `SACCR_README_sheet.png` and
`SACCR_Results.png` show sheets of the workbook built from
`src/workbook/SACCR_Template.xlsx`, with the demonstration portfolio. The owner
took them in Excel for Windows on 2026-10-16. The input sheets show the
template's own content; Results shows a run of that portfolio on that day.

- **Processing:** cropped to the sheet area (no window title, sheet tabs or
  status bar) and reduced to a 256-colour PNG palette without dithering. They
  are not otherwise edited.
- **Number format:** the values follow the regional settings of the machine
  that took them (decimal comma, thousands point); the workbook itself reads
  and writes locale-independent values.
- **Use:** the root README shows Results, and NettingSets, Trades and Params
  under "Input sheets"; the Wiki can reference any of them.
- **Results:** the image shows the columns up to the multiplier; PFE, EAD and
  the status lie to its right.

Retake a screenshot when the layout or content of its sheet changes, and keep
the same processing.
